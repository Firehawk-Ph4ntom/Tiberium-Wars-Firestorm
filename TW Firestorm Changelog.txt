=========================================================================

                      TW Firestorm V1.12 Changelog

=========================================================================

=====================================================

- New Unit/Structure and Upgrade Additions:
  > "Harrier Transport": A new air Transport unit for Mutant units trained by the Dropship Bay and Mutant Hovel!

-------------------------
-------------------------

Bug Fixes:

- Fixed all PVE Gamemode specific Units and Structures not giving off Experience

- Fixed "Tiberium Floaters" not being able to force fire on allies

- Fixed Airborne Transports providing vision to the player even if no units were garrisoned inside

- Fixed "EMP Strike" Support Power exploit where players could use hotkeys to constantly retarget the support power, acting as a free scouting power

Nod:

- Fixed "Obelisk of Light" not being able to fire on "Jetpack Troopers" or "Hive Stalkers"

- Fixed "Cyborg Gunners" and the "Cyborg Commando" voice lines not being directional audio

-------------------------
-------------------------

Health Changes:

- Adjusted the health points of various units:

  > Lowered the health of "Hive Stalkers" from 250 to 200

-------------------------
-------------------------

Weapon Changes:

- Adjusted the attack range of a few units:
  > Increased the attack range of "Photon Cannons" from 350 to 365

- Adjusted the damage of a few units:
  > Increased the damage of "Guardian Cannon's" Rail Gun from 570 to 575
  > Increased the damage of "Guardian Cannon's" Sonic Cannon from 650 to 750
  > Increased the damage of "Missile Battery's" Sonic Grenades from 325 to 400
  > Increased the damage of the "Ripple Rocket Battery's" Sonic Grenades from 350 to 400
  > Increased the damage of "Wolverine" Jump Jets when landing on enemy Infantry from 200 to 350
  > Increased the damage of the "Kodiak Battlecruiser's" AA Cannon from 150 to 200
  > Increased the damage of "Cyborg Decimators" Cluster Missiles from 200 to 300
  > Increased the damage of "Ravagers" from 125 to 175, and changed Weapon Damage Type from 'GUN' to 'SNIPER'
  > Increased the damage of "Ravagers'" "Ichor Agitation" ability from 400 to 575

  > Lowered the damage of "Predator Tank's" Sonic Cannon from 460 to 400
  > Lowered the damage of "Mammoth Tank's" Sonic Cannon from 650 to 575
  > Lowered the damage of "Colossus Walker's" Sonic Cannon from 800 to 700
  > Lowered the damage of the "MARV's" Sonic Grenades from 200 to 185
  > Lowered the damage of "Orca Fighters" from 425 to 350

  - Adjusted the damage effectiveness of AA units against "Jetpack Troopers" and "Hive Stalkers":
    > Increased the damage dealt by most AA units by +50%
    > Exceptions:
      - "Orca Battle Fortress": +70% vs. Jetpack Troopers
      - "Battlebase": +100% vs. Hive Stalkers
      - "Leviathan AA Drones": +300%

  - Adjusted the damage effectiveness of Sonic Cannons and Sonic Shells against Scrin Units and Structures:
    > Increased the damage dealt to Scrin Units from +10% to +50%
    > Reduced the damage penalty against Scrin Structures from -55% to -50%

  - Adjusted the Clip Reload Time of a few units:
    > Increased the Clip Reload Time of the "Colossus Walker's" Rail Gun from 3s to 3.3s
    > Increased the Clip Reload Time of the "MARV's" Sonic Grenades from 2s to 2.2s - 2.5s

  - Increased the "Redeemer Warmech's" damage against Aircraft by +25%

  - "Lightning Spikes" and units upgraded with the "Shock Pods" upgrade no longer deal reduced damage to Structures

  - "Cyborg Decimators'" Cluster Missiles now spawn 5 projectiles instead of 4

-------------------------
-------------------------

Object Property Changes:

- The "Cobra Commando" now benefits from the "Liquefied Contamination Mix" upgrade

- The "Reinforcement Bay" now leaves a recapturable husk when destroyed

- "Decimator Cyborgs" are now unlocked at tier 2

- "Orbital Ion Storm" Support Power now spawns in 10 Ion Storm Clouds in a randomized position instead of 3 Clouds in a fixed position

- "Shockwave Artillery" Support Power now spawns in projectiles that fall from the sky similar to Orbital Strike

- "Combat Medic Teams" now have a 8s delay before being able to deploy into a Hospital when respawning from a Hospital sold or when first trained

-------------------------
-------------------------

Misc Changes:

- Model, Animation, and ParticleFX Changes:
  > Added subtle Trail effects for all Tank Shell projectiles, including Firestorm Ignition shells having their own variant
  > Adjusted the size and transparency of all Missile projectiles
  > Changed the Trail FX of "Cyborg Decimator" Cluster missile projectiles, they are now consistent with the other projectiles
  > Changed the trail FX of "Mutant Pitbull's" Mortar Projectiles, and added a Heroic variant with Heroic FX
  > Added a trail to the Web Launchers of "Cyborg Decimators" and "Purifier Warmechs"
  > Toned down the size of "Black Hands'" flame stream effects
  > Adjusted the flame stream effects of "Flame Tanks", "Purifier Warmechs", and the "Redeemer Warmech". They now look visually consistent with the Black Hand flame stream effects, but are much bigger in size
  > Removed the Gas cloud effect from "Tiberium Troopers'" Tiberium stream
  > Adjusted the Opacity and Alpha channels of many Dirt, Dust, Steam, Smoke, and Scrin Dark Matter FX, making many effects less dense
  > Adjusted the size of the "Kodiak Battlecruiser" and "Leviathan Airship" Flying Dust FX
  > Optimized the Firing FX of the "Hammerhead Gunship"
  > Added extra Dust FX to the "Shockwave Artillery" Explosion FX
  > Removed the Liftoff Dust FX from "Venoms" when first coming out of the "Air Tower"
  > Adjusted the Size of the Dust FX emitted from moving light vehicles
  > Adjusted the size of the "Mammoth MK.II Walker" and the "Redeemer Warmech's" Footprint Dust FX
  > Adjusted the Burst Count of Nod's Decoy Army Casting FX, making it less dense
  > Added Heroic DOT Hit FX for Firestorm Ignition, and adjusted the size of the Hit FX for Infantry

  > Added Debris for "Mantis"

- Audio Changes:
  > Added new voice lines to "Cyborg Decimators"
  > Adjusted the volume of "Cyborg Gunners" and the "Cyborg Commando" voice lines

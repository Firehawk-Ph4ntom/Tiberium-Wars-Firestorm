-------------------------

Bug Fixes:

- Fixed "Avenger Tank", "Armadillo APC", "Mobile EMP Cannon", and "Beam Cannon" not properly having tread subobjects for moving left/right

-------------------------
-------------------------

Object Property Changes:

- "Propaganda Speakers" upgrade now affects "Redeemer Warmech"

-------------------------
-------------------------

Shader Changes:
  > Adjusted the Adaptive Armor, Railgun Accelerator, and Tiberium Object shaders:
    - Adaptive Armor and Railgun Accelerator:
      > The shader has been altered to utilize the House Color texture indepedent off the Specular map texture for team color display

    - Tiberium Objects:
      > When under stealth effect, the Tiberium Objects attached to units such as Crystals on the back of "Ichor Ravagers" or on upgraded "Devastator Warships" will no longer completely turn opaque, and will now instead pulse with the rest of the unit

-------------------------
-------------------------

Map Changes:

Campaign:

GDI Campaign:

- Facility of Terror:
  > Increased the AI unit build delay timers for all difficulties
  > Increased the startup delay timer for when the AI activates

- Establishing a Foothold:
  > Increased the AI unit build delay range timers for all difficulties
  > Lowered the Growth Delay of all Tiberium Fields from 8s to 7s

- Crucible of the Holy Temple:
  > The Temple of Nod no longer gets stealthed from Disruption Towers

New Unit/Structure and Upgrade Additions:
- Added 6 new Mutant units to the Dropship Bay!:
  > "Salvaged Pitbull": Tier 1 Scout unit that is effective vs. Light Vehicles and Aircraft. Has an Anti-Infantry/Anti-Structure Mortar, and can fire an EMP mortar round through an ability
  > "Salvaged Humvee": Tier 1 Anti-Infantry/Anti-Aircraft unit, has a repair drone, and can garrison an Infantry unit inside it. It is a great support unit, and has a mini-rocket pod to help with Light Vehicles
  > "Salvaged APC": Tier 1 Reckoner/Surveyor Vehicle Hybrid. It can deploy into a stationary bunker to accommodate more garrison slots, while also providing Build Radius and Stealth Detection, and can repack back up to an APC
  > "Salvaged Devils Tongue": Tier 2 Anti-Infantry/Anti-Structure/Anti-Garrison unit, functions just like the Liquifier Tank without the Subterranean move ability, but has a garrison slot
  > "Salvaged Mammoth Tank": Tier 3 Heavily armored unit similar to the Mammoth Tank, but with a garrison slot. This unit, while weaker, is much cheaper to produce and has decent Crowd Control capabilities
  > "Salvaged MLRS": Tier 3 Heavy Artillery unit. The unit is just a standard artillery tank, but unlike all the other tanks that have GRENADE damage type and are effective vs. Infantry and Structures mostly, this one can kill vehicles much more quickly, and has a decent size AoE
(The only way to unlock and use these units for now is capturing the Dropship Bay with a Mutant Salvager)

- Added a new upgrade to the "Tiberium Chemical Plant": "Liquefied Contamination Mix", which, just like the "Corrosive Ichor Mix" upgrade for Scrin, "Tiberium Troopers" and "Liquifier Tanks" now spawn Contamination Zones with their weapon, and can be further upgraded with "Blue Tiberium Core"

- Added a new upgrade to the "Stasis Chamber": "Overlord's Calling", which increases the squad size of Shock Troopers, Ravagers, and Seismic Troopers, improving their combat effectiveness

- Renamed "The Ascended" to "Cyborg Pummelers" and replaced its model with a modified "Enlightened" model from KW
  > Added the "EMP Blast" ability to the unit

- Added new Air Transports for both GDI and Nod Factions!:
  > "Condor Carryall": This Air Transport is for GDI, and unlike the V35-Ox, this Transport is more durable, but moves much slower
  > "Nightwind Transport": Nod's new Air Transport, which, unlike the Carryall, moves superbly fast, but isn't as tanky as the Carryall

- "Wolverines" can now destroy Missile and Plasma Disc projectiles at close range!

- Added new voice lines and sound effects to "Tiberian Fiends"!

- Added new variations of Crates!:
  > Firepower Crate: Units collecting this Crate will receive 25% extra attack damage
  > Armor Crate: Units collecting this Crate will receive 25% extra armor
  > Mobility Crate: Units collecting this Crate will receive 25% extra movement speed
  > Rate-Of-Fire Crate: Units collecting this Crate will receive 25% extra Fire Rate
  > Attack Range Crate: Units collecting this Crate will receive 25% extra attack and vision range
  > Cloaking Crate: Units collecting this Crate will become stealthed!
  > Tiberium Field Crate: Units colliding with this Crate will spawn a miniature Tiberium Field
(For each unit affected by power-up crates, a special icon will display over that specific unit as well)

- Added a new unit to the Dropship Bay for Nod, Terror Drones! These Tier 1 Drones are cheap, light, quick, and very pesky as they can make quick work of enemy armor! Late-game, they can gain the ability to remain stealthed while standing still, like a mine!

- The Cyborg Hijacker will be retiring from the Dropship Bay, and will be returning to Civilian Life~~ (Removed Cyborg Hijacker from the mod)

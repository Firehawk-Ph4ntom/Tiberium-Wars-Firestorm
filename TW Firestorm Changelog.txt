                      TW Firestorm V1.12 Changelog

=====================================================

- New Unit/Structure and Upgrade Additions:
  > "Harrier Transport": A new air Transport unit for Mutant units trained by the Dropship Bay and Mutant Hovel!

-------------------------
-------------------------

Weapon Changes:

- Adjusted the attack range of a few units:
  > Increased the attack range of "Photon Cannons" from 350 to 365

- Adjusted the damage of a few units:
  > Increased the damage of "Wolverine" Jump Jets when landing on enemy Infantry from 200 to 350
  > Increased the damage of "Ravagers" from 125 to 175, and changed Weapon Damage Type from 'GUN' to 'SNIPER'
  > Increased the damage of "Ravagers'" "Ichor Agitation" ability from 400 to 575

  > Lowered the damage of "Orca Fighters" from 425 to 350

-------------------------
-------------------------

Object Property Changes:

- The "Cobra Commando" now benefits from "Liquefied Contamination Mix" upgrade

- The "Reinforcement Bay now leaves a recapturable husk when destroyed"

-------------------------
-------------------------

Misc Changes:

- Model, Animation, and ParticleFX Changes:
  > Added subtle Trail effects for all Tank Shell projectiles, including Firestorm Ignition shells having their own variant
  > Adjusted the size and transparency of all Missile projectiles
  > Changed the Trail FX of "Cyborg Decimator" Cluster missile projectiles, they are now consistent with the other projectiles
  > Changed the trail FX of "Mutant Pitbull's" Mortar Projectiles, and added a Heroic variant with Heroic FX
  > Added a trail to the Web Launchers of "Cyborg Decimators" and "Purifier Warmechs"
  > Toned down the size of "Black Hands'" flame stream effects
  > Adjusted the flame stream effects of "Flame Tanks", "Purifier Warmechs", and the "Redeemer Warmech". They now look visually consistent with the Black Hand flame stream effects, but are much bigger in size
  > Removed the Gas cloud effect from "Tiberium Troopers'" Tiberium stream
  > Adjusted the Opacity and Alpha channels of many Dirt, Dust, and Smoke FX, making many effects less dense
  > Adjusted the size of the "Kodiak Battlecruiser" and "Leviathan Airship" Flying Dust FX
  > Optimized the Firing FX of the "Hammerhead Gunship"
  > Added extra Dust FX to the "Shockwave Artillery" Explosion FX
  > Removed the Liftoff Dust FX from "Venoms" when first coming out of the "Air Tower"
